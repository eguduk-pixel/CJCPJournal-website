About the journal

Journal pilot: submissions are closed. Faculty approval is pending for participation, editorial policies and publication permissions. No articles have been published.

Research across disciplines

The Student Journal of Interdisciplinary Research, known as CJCP Journal, is a school project of Central Jersey College Prep Charter School. It is being developed as a faculty-guided space for students to ask focused questions, examine evidence and communicate their findings.

The planned scope connects the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, mathematics and technology. Work that draws on more than one field is welcome within the proposed scope.

How the journal will work

The proposed path is submission, initial checks, review, revision, faculty decision, copyediting and proofs, followed by owner publication of an approved version. The policy overview explains which decisions still await faculty adoption.

Reading and participating

Public journal pages are open to everyone. Editorial participation is limited to approved CJCP students and faculty using their verified school Google accounts. A school email address alone does not grant access. Outside authors and reviewers are not part of the initial pilot.
