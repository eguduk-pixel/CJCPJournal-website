Editorial team

Journal pilot: submissions are closed. Faculty approval is pending for participation, editorial policies and publication permissions. No articles have been published.

A faculty-guided student journal

Editorial appointments have not yet been announced. The following describes the planned responsibilities, not a list of appointed editors.

  • Authors develop their work, respond to feedback and approve attribution.
  • Reviewers evaluate explicitly assigned anonymous review copies and provide constructive feedback.
  • Student editorial assistants help with assigned editorial tasks. They do not make final decisions, publish articles or administer accounts.
  • Faculty advisers and editors oversee manuscripts, make editorial decisions and approve final versions.
  • The journal owner approves membership, maintains the service and explicitly publishes faculty-approved work.

Joining the journal

Initial participation is limited to CJCP students and faculty with owner-approved accounts. Invitations, public registration and outside participation are currently closed.
